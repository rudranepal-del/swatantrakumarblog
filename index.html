Serving the Government of Nepal with dedication and purpose, I have spent over a decade working within the Ministry of Energy, Water Resources and Irrigation — an institution at the very heart of Nepal's developmental aspirations. My work spans the full spectrum of water governance: from the high Himalayan watersheds that feed our rivers, to the last-mile irrigation channels that sustain our farmers.

Nepal sits atop one of the world's greatest untapped hydropower potentials — an estimated 83,000 MW of technically feasible capacity. Yet realising this potential requires not just engineering, but visionary policy, community engagement, and environmental stewardship. This intersection of technocracy and governance is where I find my professional calling.

Through this blog, I aim to share insights from my professional journey — the policy debates I witness, the projects that shape lives, the rivers whose futures we negotiate, and my reflections on what it means to serve the public in the land of the Himalayas.
