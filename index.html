Serving the Government of Nepal with dedication and purpose, I have spent over a decade working within the Ministry of Energy, Water Resources and Irrigation — an institution at the very heart of Nepal's developmental aspirations. My work spans the full spectrum of water governance: from the high Himalayan watersheds that feed our rivers, to the last-mile irrigation channels that sustain our farmers.

Nepal sits atop one of the world's greatest untapped hydropower potentials — an estimated 83,000 MW of technically feasible capacity. Yet realising this potential requires not just engineering, but visionary policy, community engagement, and environmental stewardship. This intersection of technocracy and governance is where I find my professional calling.

"Water is not just a resource — it is the lifeblood of civilisation, and in Nepal, its stewardship is our greatest responsibility."

— Swatantra Kumar Karna

Through this blog, I aim to share insights from my professional journey — the policy debates I witness, the projects that shape lives, the rivers whose futures we negotiate, and my reflections on what it means to serve the public in the land of the Himalayas. I believe in transparent governance and the power of evidence-based policy to transform lives at scale.

Career

Professional
Journey

2020 — Present
Senior Officer
Ministry of Energy, Water Resources and Irrigation, Singha Durbar

Leading policy formulation and implementation for water resource governance, coordinating with national and international stakeholders on hydropower development and irrigation expansion programs.

2015 — 2020
Section Officer
Department of Water Resources and Irrigation (DWRI)

Managed project appraisal for medium and large irrigation schemes. Worked on inter-agency coordination for the Irrigation Master Plan and oversaw implementation monitoring across provinces.

2012 — 2015
Officer
Water and Energy Commission Secretariat (WECS)

Contributed to national water resource inventory and baseline studies. Assisted in preparation of Nepal's first comprehensive Water Resources Strategy, providing analytical support for policy briefs.

2009 — 2012
Assistant Officer
Department of Irrigation, Government of Nepal

Field postings in Madhesh Province overseeing canal rehabilitation and farmer irrigation group (FIG) strengthening programmes. Developed grassroots understanding of water user communities.
